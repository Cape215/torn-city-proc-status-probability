Basically i saw some misconception about how percentages work and decided to do this script,
for example, i read that a 10% chance of proc. is 1/10 meaning that in 1 out of 10 atacks the effect have to happen,
wich is false, you have to understand that that 10% chance is in every hit individually,
so you just have a 10% chance of having that effect happening. in the other hand if you have atacked 30 times and you didnt proc. any status effect you are quite an unlucky person.
